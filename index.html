Authorization & Use.

Strict requirements govern the operation of San Andreas State Police motorcycles.

Section 1

Purpose

Establishing guidelines for the use of the motorcycle and related equipment, including its use as an emergency vehicle in accordance with the safety of officers and the public.

Section 2

Authorization

  • Valid motorcycle endorsed driver’s license.
  • Successful completion of the authorized SASP Motorcycle Operator Training Course.
  • Operators must complete monthly in-service training to maintain certification.
Section 3

Operational Use

Officers must pass both the Track Test (bike handling, obstacle navigation, emergency braking) and the Chase Test (pursuit proficiency and adherence to policy).

Failure to maintain required standards will result in suspension of authorization until retraining is completed.

Section 4

Emergency Operation

Police motorcycle operators shall operate the motorcycle in accordance with the SASP SOP regarding Emergency Vehicle Operation.

Section 5

Vehicle Pursuits

5.1 - Restrictions

SASP motorcycles are not primary pursuit vehicles but may participate in pursuits subject to the following restrictions:

  • Only engage in a vehicle pursuit when the officer has probable cause to believe that any person in the Bike has committed (or attempted to commit) a felony involving the use (or threatened use) of force and a high probability exists that the suspect.
  • Officers operate with due regard for safety and in compliance with the "SASP Emergency Vehicle Operation Guidelines SOP".

5.2 - Bike Units & Standby

  • A maximum of two motorcycles may actively engage in a pursuit.
  • A third motorcycle may remain on standby, activated only with supervisory approval (Sergeant or higher).
  • Once a marked vehicle assumes the lead, motorcycle units must disengage unless directed otherwise by a supervisor.
  • Only one officer per motorcycle; no passengers permitted.
  • A maximum of three motorcycles may perform Code 1 operations simultaneously.
  • Only a High Command (HC) supervisor may authorize deployment if certified bike officers are unavailable.

Standards & Accountability.

Sections 6 & 7

Uniform & Inspection

Uniform: Dept. approved helmet, jacket, pants, and boots. For motorcycle duty only.

Inspection: Required before each shift. Equipment includes helmet, eye protection, leather boots, bodyworn camera, and safety equipment.

Section 8

Training Protocol

Conducted every 15 days, covering:

  • Policy and tactical reviews
  • Practical handling exercises
  • City specific hazard awareness & terrain navigation

Officers must demonstrate proficiency in all modules.

Section 9

Strike System

A three-strike accountability system applies, issued only by certified Instructors.

  • 2 Strikes: Must retake training; restricted from operation until re-certified.
  • 3 Strikes: License revoked. Officer permanently removed from motorcycle duty.
